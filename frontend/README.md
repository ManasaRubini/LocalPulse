# NammaCity 🏙️⚡
**Hyperlocal Community Civic Reporting, Interactive Discovery & AI Companion**

---

## 🌟 Overview
**NammaCity** empowers citizens in Coimbatore to report local infrastructure issues (potholes, water leaks, uncollected garbage, streetlight outages), discover nearby amenities (hospitals, police stations, water boards), track community events, and interact with **NammaCity AI** in natural English and Tanglish.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- 📊 **Live Civic Feed**: Real-time community reports with category filters, search, and upvoting.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Explore Map**: OpenStreetMap integration pinpointing hospitals, police stations, fire stations, water utilities, and reported civic hazards.
- 🎙️ **NammaCity AI Assistant**: Conversational civic companion with hardware microphone support, persistent chat history (📜), and Google Gemini 1.5 Flash reasoning.
- 📝 **Incident Reporting**: Fast photo proof attachment, GPS auto-detection, priority classification, and anonymous submission.
- 🏆 **Citizen Karma System**: Earn reputation badges and points for active community stewardship.
- 🚨 **Emergency SOS Speed-Dial**: One-tap 24/7 access to Police (100), Medical Ambulance (108), and Fire & Rescue (101).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Flutter / Dart (Material 3, flutter_map, speech_to_text, geolocator)
- **Backend**: FastAPI / Python (SQLite RAG, Overpass API, Google Gemini AI)
- **Deployment**: Render Cloud (`https://my-backend-4hfj.onrender.com`)
